# FUNDING - STM-CPM

## What Funding Covers
Preregistration, literature-search time, laboratory time/materials for E1/E2, sensor calibration, open-access publication, data archiving, independent replication support, accessibility/translation.

## What Funding Does NOT Cover
Buying results, authorship, endorsement, changes to preregistered thresholds, medical/travel/powers claims, personal payments unrelated to research, or any claim that data exist.

## How Funders Are Acknowledged
Named in FUNDING.md + CHANGELOG.md + any publication, with amount/range, purpose, and conditions, unless funder requests anonymity (then purpose + range still disclosed).

## Principles
No strings on conclusions. No embargo on null results. All funding disclosed. No funder may see data before public release except as required for audit.

## Ways to Fund
Email earthangelventuresph@gmail.com with subject `[STM-CPM Funding]` or open Issue `funding-collaboration.md`. PayPal: https://paypal.me/earthangelofficialph (for donations only - not purchase of access/results). Do not send funds to any other account claiming to be us.
