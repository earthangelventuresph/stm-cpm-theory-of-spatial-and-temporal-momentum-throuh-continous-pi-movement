# FAQ - STM-CPM v1.0

**No data have been collected. No results are reported or implied.**

1. **Is it proven?** No. P5/P6 are hypotheses. Untested. The only new claim is predictive and falsifiable.
2. **What is temporal momentum?** pt = E/c, the time component of four-momentum. Established physics, adopted as a definition. See Glossary.
3. **What is phase momentum?** Action variable J, conjugate to phase. J = Emech/ω for a harmonic oscillator.
4. **What is continuous pi movement?** Phase θ(t) continuous, differentiable, non-decreasing, advancing through successive pi-steps without jumps.
5. **What is a pi-step?** A phase advance of π radians - one half cycle - lasting τπ = π/ω, carrying action Sπ = πJ.
6. **Does π cause motion?** No. π appears because a half cycle spans π radians. The manuscript explicitly rejects any independent causal role for π.
7. **Is this parallel Earths / multiverse?** No. Nothing in this paper claims parallel Earths, alternate realities, or travel between them.
8. **Can I travel / teleport / gain powers?** No. No travel, powers, energy generation, or superluminal claim is made.
9. **Can I buy access / results / participation?** No. Nothing is for sale. Funding (FUNDING.md) does not buy results, authorship, or access.
10. **Who is the researcher?** Angelica Pangan, Lead Author, Earth Angel Ventures, Manila, Philippines - Open Internationally. ORCID 0009-0007-2793-5008.
11. **Why take this seriously?** Because it is falsifiable: thresholds, baselines, controls, and binary decisions were fixed before data. Seriousness here means testability, not proof.
12. **What is the first experiment?** E1 validates apparatus/definitions (η ≤ 0.02, ρP ≤ 0.03, Q ≥ 200). E2 runs only if E1 passes. E5 runs before E2.
13. **What would falsify it?** E2: Stage 2 lower bound Δ at δ=0.10 not > 0.05 gives P5 Not supported. E3: pooled Δ upper bound not < 0.02 gives P6a Not supported. E5: < 80% eligible cells gives robustness Not established.
14. **What if it fails?** Publish the null result. Definitions remain as reference. Claim restricted/retired per protocol. Failure is informative.
