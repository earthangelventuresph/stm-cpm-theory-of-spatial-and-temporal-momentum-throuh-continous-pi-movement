## Identity
Earth Angel Ventures is a research venture based in Manila, Philippines - Open Internationally. Research Owner for this repository. Lead Author: Angelica Pangan.

## Mission
To do work that is honest, testable, and useful - work others can examine, challenge, reproduce, and build upon. Passion | Purpose | People. Transforming the world, better than we found it.

## Vision
A body of research where the standard is not how extraordinary a claim sounds, but how reliably the evidence supports it.

## What We Do
- Publish theoretical frameworks with full definitions, equations, and preregistered falsification protocols.
- Publish protocols before data, so failure is informative.
- Publish null results when the evidence requires them.
- Disclose AI assistance, funding, interests, and limitations.

## What We Do NOT Do
- We do not claim data, results, approvals, funding, or partnerships that do not exist.
- We do not claim new physical laws, medical benefits, travel, or powers.
- We do not sell access to research, results, or participation.
- We do not present analytic illustrations as measured data.
