## Research Position

### What IS asserted
- Definitions: temporal momentum pt = E/c; phase momentum J; pi-step; continuous pi movement.
- Established properties: parity of the pi-step for symmetric potentials; action-angle relations.
- Hypothesis (P5/P6): causally tracked phase features improve forecasts of drifting oscillations, with onset near δ* = π/(8Nd), no benefit at zero drift. **Untested. No support claimed.**

### What is NOT asserted
- No new physical law. No independent causal role for π. No empirical support. No medical, travel, or powers claim. No claim that π causes motion. No claim that results extend to strongly nonlinear or chaotic systems.

## Approach
Define everything. Separate fact, definition, and hypothesis. Fix falsification thresholds before data. Use complexity-matched baselines. Publish the protocol so others can falsify it.
