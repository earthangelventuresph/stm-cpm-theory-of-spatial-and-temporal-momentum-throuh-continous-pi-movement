# CODE OF CONDUCT

## Pledge
We pledge to make participation welcoming, evidence-first, and respectful - regardless of background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Standards
- State claims with their evidence status (fact / definition / hypothesis / untested).
- Quote accurately; do not present illustrations as data.
- Criticise ideas, not people. No harassment, threats, slurs, or doxxing.
- Disclose conflicts, funding, and AI assistance where relevant.

## Enforcement
Reports to earthangelventuresph@gmail.com with subject `[Conduct]`. Maintainer will review, warn, or remove content / ban as needed, and log action without disclosing reporter identity.

## Scope
Applies to all repo spaces: Issues, PRs, discussions, and linked channels representing this project.
